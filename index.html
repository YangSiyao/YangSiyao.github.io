About

Scientific computing for high-dimensional problems.

I am a tenure-track associate professor at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science of Chinese Academy of Sciences. I am also an instructor at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS).

Research interests

My research interests lie broadly in the scientific computing for high-dimensional problems, including:

  1. 01Numerical methods for open quantum systems
  2. 02Tensor methods for data science and Monte Carlo sampling
  3. 03Modeling and numerical simulations for rarefied gas
